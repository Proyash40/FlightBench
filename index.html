About this simulator / limitations
  • Runs a JS transpiler over a common subset of Arduino C++ — not a real compiler. Structs, classes, pointers, and templates are not supported.
  • delay() is non-blocking inside setup(), loop(), and any function you define, so long choreographed sequences animate correctly without freezing the tab.
  • Drive the aircraft with drone.setThrottle()/setYaw()/setPitch()/setRoll()/arm()/disarm()/setFlightMode()/setCameraTilt()/setSpeed(), or print telemetry as Serial.println("T:80,Y:0,P:20,R:-10").
  • The chase camera, PID graph, and physics model are simplified, illustrative simulations — not certified flight-dynamics or control-system models.
FlightBench runs entirely client-side. Nothing is uploaded anywhere.
